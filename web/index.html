      Data

      • Earth NASA Blue Marble / Black Marble, GIBS (VIIRS true colour, Black Marble nightly, GOES GeoColor), EUMETSAT EUMETView (MTG GeoColour, Meteosat-9 IODC Natural Colour Enhanced)
      • Moon NASA SVS / LRO
      • Clouds NOAA/NESDIS GMGSI; Himawari-9 (JMA) via NASA GIBS, live over East Asia and the west Pacific
      • Satellites CelesTrak
      • Launches The Space Devs, SpaceX via CelesTrak
      • Aurora, Sun image, space weather, ionosphere NOAA SWPC (GloTEC, D-RAP)
      • Meteors Global Meteor Network; fireballs NASA/JPL CNEOS
      • Buoys NOAA OSMC, NOAA NDBC (incl. DART); Argo floats: the International Argo Program, via NOAA OSMC
      • Storms, floods, wildfires, eruptions GDACS, NHC
      • Volcanic ash EUMETSAT Ash RGB (Geo Ring, MSG 0° and Indian Ocean), via EUMETView
      • Sea temperature GHRSST MUR (JPL, NASA PO.DAAC), via NASA GIBS
      • Flood extent NASA LANCE VIIRS global flood product, via NASA GIBS
      • US volcano alert levels USGS Volcano Hazards Program (HANS); positions from the Smithsonian's Volcanoes of the World
      • Fires NASA FIRMS, EUMETSAT MTG Fire Radiative Power
      • Quakes USGS; EMSC for Europe and the Mediterranean
      • Lightning NOAA GOES GLM, EUMETSAT MTG Lightning Imager
      • Snow & ice NOAA IMS, NSIDC
      • Smoke NOAA HMS
      • Radar RainViewer; satellite rain NASA GPM IMERG (GIBS)
      • Tsunami NOAA NTWC/PTWC, NWS
      • Severe weather NOAA NWS, Storm Prediction Center; Europe: MeteoAlarm, data by EUMETNET members (CC BY 4.0)
      • ISS telemetry NASA ISSLive
      • ISS model NASA / Michael D. Carbajal (NASA 3D Resources)
      • Places GeoNames via Open-Meteo

      URL parameters

      viewfixed · sunrel · orbit · moon · track
      trackwhat to follow: iss, tiangong, satgus (Mark Rober's Space Selfie satellite), launch, sunrise, moonrise
      lat, lonviewer position (fixed) or offset from the subsolar point (sunrel)
      period, incorbit period (hours) and inclination (°)
      distviewer distance in Earth radii; 0 = orthographic
      mag, rotsize and rotation (°)
      waitseconds between redraws (default 60)
      time, speedISO start time; clock multiplier
      clouds, lights, grid, markers, label0 or 1
      stars1, 0, or photo (dimmed like a camera exposed for sunlit Earth)
      satsall (Starlink + named), featured, or 0
      aurora, storms, fires, quakes0 hides that layer (keys A W X Q)
      buoys, buoyBuoys on the globe, off by default (key Shift+B); buoy=46026 opens that buoy’s window. Click a buoy to open it.
      meteorsThe last day’s meteors (Global Meteor Network) and recent fireballs (NASA/JPL), off by default (key Shift+M)
      ionoIonosphere wash: tec electron content (GloTEC), drap HF radio absorption (D-RAP), or 0 (key Shift+I)
      lightning, events, snowlightning, GDACS floods/wildfires/eruptions with NASA's flood map and USGS's US volcano alert levels, snow and ice (keys Z Y I)
      sstSea temperature: temp, anomaly (El Niño, marine heatwaves; 1 means this), or 0 (key Shift+H)
      ashvolcanic ash: EUMETSAT's Ash RGB, and GDACS's eruptions in a window (key Shift+A; off by default)
      radar, smoke, tsunamiRainViewer radar, NOAA HMS smoke, NOAA tsunami alerts (keys Shift+R Shift+S Shift+T)
      imagerylive GOES and MTG pictures by day, daily yesterday’s VIIRS when zoomed in, painted neither; with live or daily, the newest night’s lights when zoomed in (key Shift+P; the older truecolor=0/1 still works)
      severeSevere weather: NWS warning polygons and the SPC day-1 outlook (US), MeteoAlarm's warnings (Europe) (key Shift+W)
      imergWith the radar, NASA IMERG satellite rain where there’s no radar (Shift+R’s middle step turns it off)
      reliefterrain lit by the real Sun, from NOAA ETOPO 2022 (key Shift+E); 0 shows NASA's fixed shaded relief
      reliefscaleterrain relief strength, 0.1–5 (default 1)
      issliveNASA ISS telemetry panel: track (while tracking the ISS), 1 (always) or 0 (key Shift+L)
      isscam1 opens the camera in the ISS telemetry: the station from 120 m above, its solar arrays posed from the live telemetry, and the Earth below (the wheel zooms it, 30–120°)
      firehoursshow fires detected within this many hours, 1–24 (default 12)
      launches0 hides launch mode (key U)
      bodies, bodyscaleSun and Moon on/off; enlarge their discs (1 = true size)
      fps1 shows the frame-rate display (key D)
      feeds1 shows the data-feeds dashboard: each source's last update and next check (key Shift+U)
      cloudsrc, satsrcfolders holding each latest.json
